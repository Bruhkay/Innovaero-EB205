One skin bay, with and without the piezo shunt

The skin between two frames and two stringers vibrates when engine sound hits it. The PZT patches (outlined) turn that motion into charge; the tuned RL circuit turns it into heat. Both bays get the same sound; the motion is slowed down and exaggerated, at the same scale on both sides.
