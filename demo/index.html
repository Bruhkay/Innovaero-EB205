EB205

A quieter VIP cabin for the Boeing 787-9

Engine noise and the turbulent boundary layer shake the composite skin; the sidewall lets part of that energy into the cabin. We stack three treatments on the sidewall and model each one from the engine to the passenger's ear.

1 · Passive layers

Damping layer on the skin, denser blanket and a heavy septum in the cavity. Works at every frequency and costs mass.

2 · Shunted piezo patches

PZT patches on each skin bay, each wired to a tuned RL circuit that turns the bay's vibration into heat at its resonance.

3 · Active trim panel

Piezo actuators on the trim panel and error microphones at head height; an FxLMS controller cancels the engine tones around the head.
