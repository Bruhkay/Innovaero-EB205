Listen to the cabin

What the passenger at the window seat on the engine side hears at cruise, for each solution. Press play, then switch between solutions; playback continues from the same moment, so you can compare directly.

Relative levels. All files share one gain, so the differences you hear are the model's differences (synthesised band levels within ±0.6 dB of the model). The absolute loudness on your headphones cannot be guaranteed: set the volume once on the baseline and then leave it. Use headphones; laptop speakers cannot play the 50–125 Hz bands that dominate cabin noise.
