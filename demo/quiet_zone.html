Quiet zone of the active trim panel

Seen from above: the trim panel runs along the bottom edge, the passenger's head (circle) sits m into the cabin. Four PZT actuators on the panel and four error microphones around the head cancel the engine tone. The quiet zone is where the reduction is at least 10 dB.
